01 MSGCN Related work

01 / RESEARCH DETAILMSGCN: fusion within and across signals

Challenge
Different feature spaces and limited labels make simple feature concatenation insufficient for representing modality relationships.
Approach
First fused DE and PSD similarity within signals, then diffused cross-signal similarity and classified the resulting graph with a GCN.
Contribution
Developed graph fusion and semi-supervised models and supported certification and intellectual-property outputs.
