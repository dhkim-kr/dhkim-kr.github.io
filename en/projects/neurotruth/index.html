01 / RESEARCH DETAILNeuroTruth: sensing, state checks and dialogue

Problem / context
Wearable predictions must trigger state checks and notifications while keeping dialogue separate from risk decisions.
Approach
Backend rules determine risk levels and notification timing. The app supports state confirmation, questionnaires and speech interaction.

02 / RESEARCH DETAILNeuroTruth: integration and notification latency

Problem / context
Confirmed the connection between classification, risk decisions, notifications and dialogue in 21 of 21 repeated integration tests.
Approach
A separate camera test processed 10 of 10 static videos. Mean processing time for a 20-second video was 14.8 seconds.
Contribution / next steps
Next steps are MBID scoring and intervention evaluation, with clinical assessment of approximately 80 participants at two hospitals planned.
Model specification and evaluation 1
Measurement scope Interpretation
Integration tests Repeated functional tests excluding MBID
Latency Notification delivery; excludes LLM response
Pending evidence MBID and clinical evaluation results
Evaluation conditions and scopeLOSO balanced accuracy: 71%; macro F1: 70%. Mean notification delay: 78.0 ms over Wi-Fi and 121.1 ms over LTE, excluding LLM response time. Camera throughput does not establish heart-rate accuracy or clinical benefit.
