Problem / context

Approach
Connected Reference and RAS coordinates through landmark and surface registration and propagated probe transforms to image slices in real time.

02 / RESEARCH DETAILStage movement, waveform acquisition and field maps

Problem / context
Stage movements accumulate waiting time, and each waveform must remain associated with its measurement location.
Approach
Adjusted VXM movement intervals and waiting times and generated plane-wise field maps from grid measurements.
Contribution / next steps
Connected robotic movement, waveform acquisition and field visualization within 3D Slicer.
