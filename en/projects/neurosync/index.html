01 / RESEARCH DETAILNeuroSync: intake, retrieval and clinician handoff

Problem / context
Free dialogue must populate clinical slots without allowing unsupported values into the handoff record.
Approach
A rule-based state machine runs 10 specialist agents. Scales use deterministic scoring, and final records undergo source and evidence checks.
Contribution / next steps
Implemented orchestration, RAG and verification logic and integrated the patient app, clinician interface and backend.
Model specification and evaluation 1
Component Implementation scope
Korean models Solar Pro 3, K-EXAONE, A.X K1
Retrieval corpus 1,248 clinical cases, 1,789 QA entries
Services FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Expo, Next.js
Evaluation conditions and scopeThe architecture and screens document an AI Champion prototype for clinician handoff support. It does not establish diagnoses.

02 / RESEARCH DETAILNeuroSync: output reliability and inference cost

Problem / context
Verification blocked 337 unsupported candidates before final output and improved slot coverage and scale assignment.
Approach
The pre-filter unsupported-candidate ratio rose from 46.4% to 55.2%, exposing limitations at the generation stage despite final-output verification.
Contribution / next steps
Further work concerns reducing calls and tokens and obtaining specialist assessment and clinical proof-of-concept evidence.

Evaluation conditions and scope384 synthetic sessions in total, with 144 in the direct comparison. Slot coverage improved from 55.6% to 98.4%; cumulative turn waiting time increased from 4.16 to 9.04 seconds. These are synthetic evaluations, not clinical outcomes.
