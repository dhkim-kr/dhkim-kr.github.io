Estimated blood pressure from two chair BCG channels using a CNN, BiGRU and temporal attention.

01 / RESEARCH DETAILTwo-channel BCG model and real-time service

Challenge
Temporal BCG information from the chair back and seat must be connected to real-time blood-pressure display.
Approach
Extracted local features from 10-second windows with a CNN, then used a BiGRU and temporal attention for regression.
Contribution
Co-developed synchronized acquisition, the blood-pressure model and the real-time analysis service.
Model specification in the released final code
Final code specification Implementation
Input 250 Hz, 2 channels, 10 seconds
CNN 10 layers; 64 / 128 / 256 / 512 channels
Regression BiGRU 64 + temporal attention
Evaluation conditions and scopeFinal code: 10 convolutional layers, 64/128/256/512 channels and a 64-unit BiGRU. The presentation figure shows an earlier configuration.

02 / RESEARCH DETAILBlood-pressure tracking and reproduction conditions

Experiment
Recorded resting and post-exercise states for about three hours per participant, with cuff references every two minutes.
Evaluation
Evaluated SBP and DBP errors for six participants using within-person temporal splits of 29/14/57%.
