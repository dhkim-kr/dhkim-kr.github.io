01 / RESEARCH DETAILCandidate physiomarkers of individual craving responses

Challenge
Craving responses differ in magnitude and direction across individuals, limiting a single marker's explanatory power.
Approach
Synchronized video stimuli, questionnaires and physiological signals, then compared individual correlations and changes across craving levels.
Contribution
Designed acquisition and quality checks at two hospitals. Conducted LMM and SHAP analyses in the expanded cohort.
Acquisition and analysis scope
Research scope Scale / analysis
Full acquisition More than 100 patients and controls
Expanded study 50 participants with AUD, 1,697 trials
Feature analysis 10-second windows; 152 physiological features
Evaluation conditions and scopeFigures: an 11-person biomarker study and a six-person heart-rate study. Expanded analysis: 50 patients with AUD and 1,697 trials.
