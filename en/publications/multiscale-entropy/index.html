Multiscale Distribution Entropy Analysis of Short Epileptic EEG Signals

Preserved samples through moving averages and compared multiscale distribution entropy for state separation in short EEG.

Published

Venue
Mathematical Biosciences and Engineering
Author role
Co-first author

Research approach

Preserved samples through moving averages and compared multiscale distribution entropy for state separation in short EEG.

Results and evaluation scope

Evaluated scale-wise ROC AUC on Bonn and Bern-Barcelona EEG, focusing on sample loss at larger scales.

Paper specification and results
Samples at scale s For a sequence of length N
Coarse-graining floor(N / s)
Moving average N - s + 1
Evaluation State separation in public EEG
InterpretationThe Bonn A segment is a five-second interval centered on the first quartile. ROC AUC measures state separation in public EEG datasets. Bern-Barcelona evaluation distinguishes focal from non-focal EEG.
