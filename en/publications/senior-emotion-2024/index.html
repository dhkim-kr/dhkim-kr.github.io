Emotion Recognition based on Multimodal Graph Representation Learning of Senior Utterance

Poster presentation

Best Poster Award

Lab publication record

Research approach

Collected 1,512 utterances spanning seven emotions from 24 participants aged 66 to 81. Fused speech and text similarity graphs with SNF, classified them with a GCN and assessed transcription errors.

Results

Subject-independent four-fold evaluation: weighted F1 was 92.33% for speech with original text and 81.38% for speech with transcribed text. Adding speech did not recover the loss from transcription errors under this protocol.
