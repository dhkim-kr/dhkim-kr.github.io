Deep learning based blood pressure estimation using ballistocardiogram

Oral presentation

Venue
30th Joint Conference on Signal Processing
Author role
Fourth author
Year
2020.09

Authors You-Jung Seo, Won-Gyu Lee, Keon-Young Kho, Dae-Hyun Kim, Hyun-woo Cho, Jae-Hyuk Choi, Jaewon Yu, Ryang-hee Sohn, Cheol-Soo Park

Lab publication record

Research approach

Filtered two-channel BCG from chair-mounted PVDF sensors at 4–15 Hz. Combined a 1D CNN and bidirectional GRU to estimate blood pressure without handcrafted pulse features.

Results

Collected about three hours of resting and post-exercise recordings from each of five participants. Trained and evaluated within each participant using cuff blood pressure as the reference.
