Contactless Blood Pressure Estimation based on Graph Convolution Network with Semi-supervised Learning

Oral presentation

Venue
IEIE Autumn Annual Conference
Author role
Second author
Year
2025.11

Authors Seo Hyun Jho, Dae Hyeon Kim, Young-Seok Choi

Lab publication record

Research approach

Extracted rPPG with POS and combined a representative pulse segment with 51 blood-pressure features. A two-layer GCN estimates systolic and diastolic pressure from a sample-similarity graph.

Results

On Vital Videos VV-Medium, which contains 250 participants, rPPG inputs yielded SBP/DBP MAE of 17.42/10.94 mmHg. Skin-color inputs yielded 18.10/11.13 mmHg.
