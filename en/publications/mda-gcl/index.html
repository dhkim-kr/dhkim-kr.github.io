Research approach

Combined topology-aware modality fusion, graph contrastive learning and adversarial domain adaptation for subject and session shifts.

Results and evaluation scope

SEED-IV accuracy: 76.1 ± 7.4% across subjects and 83.8 ± 8.3% across sessions. Evaluation is transductive and uses unlabeled target features.
