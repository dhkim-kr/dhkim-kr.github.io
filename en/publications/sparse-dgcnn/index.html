Enhancing EEG-Based Emotion Recognition Using Sparse Dynamic Graph CNN with ℓ2,1-Norm

Research approach

Applied row-wise ℓ₂,₁ sparsity and proximal updates to a learned EEG electrode graph.

Results and evaluation scope

With DE features, subject-independent accuracy is 78.36 ± 9.53% on SEED and 47.67 ± 6.67% on SEED-IV. The public synthetic example does not reproduce the benchmark results.
