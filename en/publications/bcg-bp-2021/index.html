Blood Pressure Estimation based on Real-Time Deep Learning Analysis on BCG

Oral presentation

Venue
KOSOMBE Spring Conference
Author role
First author
Year
2021.05

Authors Dae Hyeon Kim, Hyeonwoo Cho, Keonyoung Ko, Jaehyeok Choi, Youjung Seo, Jaewon Yu, Ryanghee Son, Cheolsoo Park

Lab publication record

Research approach

Recorded two-channel BCG at 250 Hz from a chair back and seat. A CNN, BiGRU and temporal attention process ten-second windows to estimate systolic and diastolic blood pressure.

Results

Six participants, with approximately three hours of resting and post-exercise measurements each. Within-person temporal splits of 29:14:57 yielded mean SBP/DBP MAE of 4.70/3.83 mmHg and RMSE of 6.02/4.90 mmHg. The study used overlapping windows and normalization before splitting.
