L2,1-norm-based Sparse Dynamic Graph Convolution Neural Network

Poster presentation

Venue
IEIE Summer Annual Conference
Author role
Second author
Year
2024.06

Authors Min Seok Lee, Dae Hyeon Kim, Young-Seok Choi

DBpia

Research approach

Applied row-wise L2,1 sparsity to a DGCNN that learns EEG electrode connections. Classified three emotions using DE features from five frequency bands and compared L1 regularization.

Results

Subject-dependent evaluation on SEED, with 15 participants: L2,1 accuracy 75.52 ± 8.88%, L1 75.56 ± 8.89% and DGCNN 74.90 ± 8.20%. Classification performance was comparable to L1.
