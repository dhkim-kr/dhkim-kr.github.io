Quantitative EEG based Emotion Recognition Using Graph Convolutional Neural Network

Oral presentation

Venue
IEIE Summer Annual Conference
Author role
First author
Year
2022.06

Authors Dae-Hyeon Kim, Young-Seok Choi

DBpia

Research approach

Decomposed EEG into five intrinsic mode functions and extracted dynamic differential entropy (DDE). Smoothed features with a moving average and classified emotions using a spectral GCN with learned electrode connections.

Results

Subject-dependent evaluation on SEED with 15 participants: DDE with moving averages reached 76.13 ± 4.91% accuracy, compared with 73.39 ± 6.24% for conventional DE with moving averages.
