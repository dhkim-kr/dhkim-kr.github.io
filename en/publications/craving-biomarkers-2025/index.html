Correlation Analysis of Biomarkers using Multimodal Biosignals for Detecting Craving States in Patients with Substance Use Disorder

Poster presentation

Venue
IEIE Autumn Annual Conference
Author role
Second author
Year
2025.11

Authors Min Seok Lee, Dae Hyeon Kim, Dong Hyuk Lee, Joon-Young Lee, Mina Kwon, Ji Eun Han, Jae Yeon Hwang, Ji-hye Kim, Sang-Kyu Lee, Moo Eob Ann, Jae Reen Jang, Young-Seok Choi

DBpia

Research approach

Synchronized ECG, PPG and skin conductance with craving ratings during VR stimuli in 11 patients with alcohol use disorder. Analyzed individual correlations with heart rate, HRV, pulse transit time and skin-conductance responses.

Results

Selected 25 candidate features from individual correlations. ECG- and PPG-derived heart-rate and NN-interval measures were prominent, with differences in response magnitude and direction across participants.
