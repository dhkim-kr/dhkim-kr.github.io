Robust Deepfake Detection using a 1D Convolution-Transformer Hybrid Model based on Remote Photoplethysmography Signals

Oral presentation

Venue
IEIE Autumn Annual Conference
Author role
Fourth author
Year
2025.11

Authors Seung Won Kim, Kyung Jun Moon, Gyeong Bin Ma, Dae Hyeon Kim, Young-Seok Choi

DBpia

Research approach

Isolated skin regions with BiSeNet and extracted rPPG with PhysNet. A 1D CNN captures local temporal features and a Transformer models global dependencies to classify real and synthetic videos.

Results

Experiments used FaceForensics++ and CelebV-HQ with a 6:2:2 train/validation/test split. Three convolutional blocks reached 88.58% accuracy, compared with 84.95% for the Transformer alone.
