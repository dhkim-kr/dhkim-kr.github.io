Remote Heart Rate Estimation from Facial Videos with Balanced Contrastive Learning

Oral presentation

Venue
ICEIC 2025
Author role
Second author
Year
2025.01
DOI GitHub

Research approach

Extracted global and local pulse representations with a 3D CNN. Balanced contrastive pairs align global and local representations within a video and separate global representations from different videos.

Results

Intra-dataset MAE/RMSE: 0.13/0.36 bpm on PURE and 1.08/2.49 bpm on UBFC-rPPG. The study used 75% of subjects for training and 25% for testing, excluding videos with heart rates of 135 bpm or higher.
