Facial Video-based Contactless Blood Pressure Estimation Using End-to-End Graph Convolutional Network

Poster presentation

Venue
IEIE Summer Annual Conference
Author role
First author
Year
2024.06

Authors Dae Hyeon Kim, Young-Seok Choi

Lab publication record

Research approach

Divided facial skin regions into five-second clips and converted RGB to YUV. A two-layer GCN estimates blood pressure from a sample-similarity graph of skin-color changes without a separate pulse-extraction stage.

Results

Transductive evaluation on VV-Medium with 250 participants and a subject-independent 70:30 split. Skin-color inputs yielded SBP/DBP MAE of 17.66/10.01 mmHg; rPPG inputs yielded 19.68/11.67 mmHg.
