01 / RESEARCH

Research focus

01

Graph representations and structure

Adaptive sample and channel graphs, semi-supervised contrastive learning and structural sparsity for limited labels and noisy relationships.

02

Multimodal learning and domain adaptation

Relation-aware fusion of biosignals, speech and language. Cross-modal alignment addresses missing inputs and subject or session changes.

03

Efficient models and evidence-based agents

Compact temporal models and shared graph operators, with observation-based state representations for retrieval and multi-agent execution.
